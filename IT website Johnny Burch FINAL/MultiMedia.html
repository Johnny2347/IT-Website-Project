Poster

Above is the poster I created using photoshop, the design is a different version of the poster for the movie jaws.
To start with I Gathered multiple different images from the internet, such as a shark, a boat, and an image of the sea, I then
imported these images into photoshop, each one on a seperate layer, I used the remove background tool to get rid of the background
of the boat and the shark to make them easier to blend into the poster. I used the image of the sea as the main background for the
poster, I then positioned the shark and boat and used the gradient tool to blend them into the image by adding a layer mask and gradient.

Once the gradient was used to blend them in, I added two different adjustment layers, one for hue and one for saturation, I adjusted these
to give the poster a slightly red colour and darker saturation. The adjustment layers helped the images appear closer in color to each
other making them blend in more seamlessly. Once the adjustment layers were added I added red text displaying 'Jaws' I chose an appropriate
size and font for the poster. At first it was challenging to find a way to blend together my seperate images, I tried out multiple different
sharks and different sea backgrounds, the main struggle was the color of the sea not matching the colour of the sharks background, I decided
to remove the sharks background to make it easier to manipulate.

Audio clip

The audio above is a short minute long clip to 'promote' the film, I made this audio clip using
audacity and various different sound effects I found online, I imported the effects into audacity
and added them to different layers. The clip features an audio recording from a storm, providing
ambient wind noises, as well as rain effects and ocean wave effects. These effects are panned in
different directions, in order to create the illusion of depth and spatial audio, the music is on
its own layer, slightly louder than the other layers to grab the listeners attention. The voiceover
is a sample I cut from the films original trailer, there are multiple different layers, most effects
are on their own layer, there is also a foghorn sound effect, and at the end of the clip, a scream then
a loud thunder crash for dramatic effect.

In the end the clip had 7 layers, at first it took a lot of
adjustment as some effects were too quiet and needed to be turned up, whilst others were too loud,
I also had to adjust the timings of the effects to ensure they lined up with each other, in the end I
was satisfied with the final clip and I embedded it into my website, it now autoplays when the multimedia
section is visited.


Vector Graphic

The logo on the home page of this website is an SVG, I created it using a free online SVG editor
called Boxy SVG Editor. The logo is made up of various different basic shapes, as well as text, I used the
editors built in shapes to construct the logo, I also added a curve to the logo by connecting multiple
vertices into the desired shape and then closing them in a loop.
